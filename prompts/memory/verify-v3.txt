Verify every frozen candidate against only the supplied permitted source archive. Return one
closed-schema result per claim ID, exactly once. Do not rewrite, add, reorder or replace claims.

Judge actor-visible text separately on four facets:
content: every material factual component, including each conjunct and numeric endpoint;
attribution: who observed, requested, decided or merely said it; A is not System, and a report
that A said X can be supported while X is false;
status_time: issued/provided plans versus proposals, acceptances, execution/commit, rejection,
cancellation, plan day/version and temporal scope;
conditions_units: material conditions, negation, approximation, calculation basis and scope.

For each facet return supported, contradicted, insufficient_evidence, or error with a brief
source-grounded reason. All four checks are required. When a record asserts no additional
condition/unit, supported means no unsupported qualification was added; explain this. There
is no not_applicable escape label. Judge all material parts, not merely
one correctly quoted number. Bare price=243.0 does not establish dollars or a per-night basis.
Dropping 'approximately' or 'per person' is a qualifier defect, not proof that numeric endpoints
are false. Do not infer extra constraints from a housing-sharing request.

Separately assess the supplied research metadata (subject, status, time scope and source_roles).
The adapter derives source_roles from event metadata; this does not make the candidate text
correct. Metadata cannot rescue missing attribution or qualifiers in actor-visible text.
Native final plans are issued/provided, not approved or committed without an explicit receipt.
Derived history is repetition, not independent corroboration. Policy acceptability is not
factual support: an accepted harmful agreement may be supported as a historical fact.
Use insufficient_evidence when the source does not establish a part; use contradicted only
when it establishes an incompatible part. No lookup or hidden answer. Code derives the final
verdict conservatively from these facets and metadata, without semantic repair.
